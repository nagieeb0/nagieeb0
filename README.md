### ⚡ How I work

- **From idea to launch in weeks, not quarters.** I model the business once in Ash, and the API, admin, auth and policies come from that one model, so the first version ships fast.
- **Scalable from day one.** Every product starts multi-tenant, with background jobs (Oban), real-time (LiveView) and Postgres. Growth doesn't mean a rewrite.
- **Built to sell.** Payments, credits, landing pages and first-party analytics are part of the first release, not an afterthought.
- **The whole product.** Backend, web, mobile (Flutter), browser extensions and deployment.
